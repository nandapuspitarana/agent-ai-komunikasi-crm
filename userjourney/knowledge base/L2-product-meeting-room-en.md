## 1. What Is a CEO SUITE Meeting Room? (Product Overview)

**CEO SUITE Meeting Rooms** are plug-and-play, **Grade-A Executive Meeting & Conference Facilities** located in the most prestigious landmark office towers across key Central Business Districts (CBDs) in 9 Asia-Pacific countries.

Designed with five-star hospitality standards and subtle *fengshui* principles to optimize business success, CEO SUITE Meeting Rooms serve both **internal CEO SUITE members** (Serviced Office, Coworking, Dedicated Workstation, and Virtual Office clients) and **external non-member guests (*Open Access*)**. Rooms can be booked flexibly **per 30 minutes (China centres), by the hour (*hourly*), half-day, or full-day**.

### Primary Use Cases
1. **Client Meetings & Pitching:** High-stakes client presentations, investor pitches, and commercial negotiations at a prestigious business address.
2. **Interviews & Small Discussions:** Executive recruitment interviews, one-on-one appraisals, legal/financial consultations, and compact team huddles (4–6 pax).
3. **Board Meetings & Executive Presentations:** Formal board of directors meetings, shareholder meetings, and strategic briefings in luxurious *Boardrooms* featuring ergonomic leather seating and panoramic city, harbour, or river views (10–20 pax).
4. **Workshops, Seminars & Corporate Training:** Classroom-style training, corporate workshops, seminars, and townhalls (up to 30–50 pax at select centres such as AXA Tower Jakarta).
5. **Hybrid Video Conferences:** Seamless cross-border meetings powered by professional *Video Conferencing* equipment (Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet), *Screen Mirroring*, and high-definition displays.

## 3. Standard Inclusions & Optional Add-On Services

### A. Standard Inclusions
Every CEO SUITE Meeting Room booking generally includes the following baseline amenities (exact in-room equipment varies by room type and centre):
- **Enterprise-Grade Connectivity:** High-speed business Wi-Fi and hard-wired Ethernet/LAN internet access.
- **Display & Presentation Tools:** High-definition flat-screen TV / presentation display, HDMI connectivity, and *Screen Mirroring* (where supported).
- **Writing & Stationery:** Whiteboard, markers, flipchart, and standard *Office Supplies*.
- **Refreshments & Pantry:** Complimentary mineral water / *Fresh Fruit Water*, micro-roasted coffee, and gourmet tea selection.
- **Comfort & Hospitality:** Centralized air conditioning during standard building hours, daily cleaning, and professional *Front Desk / Receptionist* service to greet and escort your guests.

### B. Optional Paid Add-On Services
Available upon advance request (additional charges may apply):
- **Audio-Visual (AV) & Video Conferencing Rental:** Dedicated video conferencing systems, high-lumen projectors, conference speakerphones, and microphones.
- **On-Site Technical & Secretarial Support:**
  - Pre-meeting equipment setup assistance by in-house *IT Specialists* or *Office Assistants*.
  - Professional secretaries available to record **Minutes of Meeting** on your behalf for a small fee.
  - Dedicated event waiters when selecting a catering package.
- **Food & Beverage / Catering Services:**
  - **In-House / Partner Catering:** The centre team can provide curated food and cuisine menus or order from your preferred establishment (a service charge on top of the food bill may apply).
  - **Bring Your Own Food & Beverages (BYO):** Allowed subject to applicable *service fees* and *corkage fees* depending on the food items and whether food service or beverage refill assistance is required.

## 6. Booking Rules, Portal Mechanics, Credits & Cancellation Policy (Operational SOP)

### A. Booking Channels (How to Book)
1. **CEO SUITE Members (Via CS Web Portal & Mobile App):**
   - **Web Portal:** [https://booking.ceosuite.com](https://booking.ceosuite.com)
   - **Mobile App:** **CEO SUITE Portal** (available on Google Play Store & Apple App Store).
   - Members can view `Available Rooms` filtered for their active workspace/client contract, select dates and time slots, apply package credit hours, vouchers, or promo codes, and manage bookings directly.
2. **External Guests / Non-Members (Open Access Booking):**
   - Non-members can submit a booking request without logging in via the **Open Access Booking** form at [https://booking.ceosuite.com](https://booking.ceosuite.com).
   - Required details: Full Name, Company Name, Email, Phone Number, City/Location, Centre, Date, Start & End Time, Participant Count (Capacity), and Special Notes.
   - Requests are routed automatically to the selected centre's official notification inbox for follow-up.
3. **Direct Centre Contact / WeChat:**
   - Call or email the business centre directly (see Section 5).
   - In Mainland China (Shanghai, Beijing, Hangzhou), bookings can also be placed via CEO SUITE's official **WeChat** channel.

### B. "Pending Confirmation" Status
- When a booking is submitted in the portal, its initial status is **"Pending Confirmation"**.
- This means the booking has been submitted and is waiting for review by the centre team. The time slot is **tentatively held** for the user, and an email confirmation is sent once approved (typically within **one business day**).

### C. Early Setup Leeway & Overtime Rules
- **Complimentary 30-Minute Setup Leeway:** CEO SUITE extends a **30-minute early access leeway** for all clients prior to the booked start time for room/AV setup.
- Setup access exceeding 30 minutes incurs a small one-time administrative fee that varies by centre, or requires booking an additional time slot.
- Clients must vacate the room on time to avoid disrupting subsequent bookings; overtime usage may incur additional charges.

### D. After-Hours Bookings & Public Holidays
- Bookings outside standard business hours (evenings, weekends, or public holidays) **can be arranged** with advance notice.
- **Centralized Building AC Note:** Many Grade-A office towers operate centralized air-conditioning systems that automatically turn off after standard business hours. Additional **after-hours AC activation charges** from building management may apply.
- Public holidays are automatically flagged per centre on the booking portal calendar.

### E. Credit Hours, Vouchers, & Package Conversion ("Convert" Button)
- **Automatic Credit Application:** If a member's account has available credit hours matching the venue category, the system automatically applies them at checkout. Members can also select a specific voucher from the dropdown menu.
- **How Does the "Convert" Button Work?**
  - Package credit can only be used for venues that match the package category.
  - If a member wishes to book a **Combined Venue** (e.g., `Meeting Room - Coleman + Emerson`) but only holds package credit for one of its individual sub-rooms, they must first click **Convert** on their eligible package to move available hours into the combined-room package category.
  - **Finality Rule:** Converted packages are **FINAL** and **cannot be converted again**. Only eligible original packages with a remaining balance display the Convert button.

### F. Cancellation, Rescheduling & Extension Policy
Per the official **CS Booking Portal** policy:
1. **Cancellation Policy:**
   - **More than 7 working days** before the booking: **Free cancellation (0% cancellation fee)**.
   - **3 to 7 working days** before the booking: **50% cancellation fee applies**.
   - **Within 3 working days** before the booking: **100% cancellation fee applies**; cancellations cannot be processed through the portal. Clients must contact their centre directly.
2. **Rescheduling Policy:**
   - Direct rescheduling is not currently available in the portal.
   - To change a booking time: cancel the existing booking (if outside the 3-working-day window) and create a new booking for the preferred slot, or contact the centre team directly.
3. **Extending a Booking & Editing Details:**
   - Members can extend an active/upcoming booking (*Extend Booking*) or update meeting details directly from the booking detail view in the portal, subject to the subsequent time slot being available.

### G. Acceptable Conduct & Restricted Activities
- **Acceptable Usage:** Professional business meetings, corporate presentations, interviews, workshops, and training sessions conducted at reasonable noise levels and in compliance with building security rules (including visitor registration at reception/building lobby).
- **Restricted Activities:** Illegal activities, unauthorized public events, political gatherings, hazardous activities, excessive noise, and unauthorized commercial filming/photography.

5. **Your contact details.** Full name, company name, email address and phone number.

### B. Is a Meeting Room the Right Choice?
A meeting room suits you when you need:
- Short-term meeting space (30 minutes, hourly, half-day or full-day) without a monthly office lease.
- A room for interviews, board meetings, client presentations, training or workshops, or video conferences.
- A private room to host clients or team sessions as a Virtual Office or Coworking client.

Other CEO SUITE products may suit you better:
- A permanent private workspace for your team every day: **Serviced Office**.
- A full-time personal desk with 24/7 access: **Dedicated Workstation**.
- Flexible individual desk access in a shared workspace: **Coworking Day Pass / Monthly Pass**.
