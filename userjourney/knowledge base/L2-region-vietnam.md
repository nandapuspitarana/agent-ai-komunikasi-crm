## Business Environment
Vietnam is a fast-growing ASEAN market with strong activity in manufacturing, technology, trade, logistics, consumer goods, finance and professional services. Hanoi is important for government and northern Vietnam business; Ho Chi Minh City is a major commercial and financial hub.

## Company Incorporation Overview
Foreign investors may need investment registration, enterprise registration and sector-specific approvals depending on activity and ownership. Market access conditions, licensing and capital requirements can vary. Do not promise company registration or licensing approval without current local guidance.

## Language
Vietnamese is the official language and primary language for government processes. English is used in many international business settings, especially in major cities, but official documents commonly require Vietnamese.

## Currency
Vietnamese dong (VND).

## Public Holidays
Public holidays commonly include New Year, Tet/Lunar New Year, Hung Kings Commemoration Day, Reunification Day, International Labour Day and National Day. Tet is the major annual holiday period and can significantly affect business schedules. Check the official annual calendar for exact dates and holiday duration.

## Payment Methods
Common business payment methods include bank transfer, corporate card where accepted, local electronic payment rails in some contexts and invoice settlement. Exact accepted methods, VAT/tax handling, deposits and billing cycles must come from CEO SUITE commercial rules or live billing data.

## Business Culture
Business culture is relationship-oriented, practical and increasingly international in major cities. Users may expect responsive follow-up, local-language support for official matters and clear explanation of what is confirmed versus pending approval.

## Compliance Considerations
Verify investment registration, enterprise registration, tax/VAT, labour/work permit, sector licensing, data/privacy, address use and local authority requirements. Do not provide legal or tax advice.

## Visa Overview
Foreign nationals working in Vietnam generally require appropriate visas, work permits or exemptions depending on role and duration. Business visits and employment are different categories.
