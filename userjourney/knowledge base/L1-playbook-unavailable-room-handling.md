# Unavailable Room Handling

## Goal
Guide consistent AI conversation handling while relying on product, centre, country, policy and live data for factual answers.

## Flow
1. Identify the user intent and required product or centre.
2. Ask only for missing details needed to proceed.
3. Retrieve the relevant KB file and live data where required.
4. Provide a concise answer with next best action.
5. Escalate when the request involves pricing exceptions, legal/tax advice, complaints, unavailable inventory, or policy ambiguity.

## What Not To Say
- Do not guarantee immediate availability.
- Do not guarantee company registration, visa, government or compliance approval.
- Do not provide legal or tax advice.
- Do not promise exact pricing, promotions or contract terms without approved live data.
