# Product: Meeting Room

## Product Summary

Meeting Rooms provide professional spaces for:

- client meetings

- interviews

- board meetings

- workshops

- training sessions

- presentations

- video conferences

Meeting Rooms are generally available on hourly, half-day or full-day booking basis.
