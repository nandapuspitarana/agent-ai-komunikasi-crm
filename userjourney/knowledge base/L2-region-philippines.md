## Business Environment
The Philippines is a major services, outsourcing, finance, technology, consumer and regional support market. Metro Manila, especially Makati, is a key corporate and financial district for local and international businesses.

## Company Incorporation Overview
Company registration commonly involves the Securities and Exchange Commission for corporations and other agencies depending on structure and activity. Tax, local government, sector licensing and foreign ownership rules may apply. Do not promise registration or ownership eligibility without current official guidance.

## Language
Filipino and English are official languages. English is widely used in business, contracts, government filings and professional services.

## Currency
Philippine peso (PHP).

## Public Holidays
Public holidays commonly include New Year's Day, Holy Week holidays, Araw ng Kagitingan, Labour Day, Independence Day, National Heroes Day, Bonifacio Day, Christmas and Rizal Day, plus Islamic holidays and special non-working days announced by government. Dates and special holidays can change annually, so check the official holiday proclamation.

## Payment Methods
Common business payment methods include bank transfer, corporate card, cheque in some B2B contexts, e-wallet or instant payment rails where accepted and invoice settlement. Exact accepted methods, VAT/tax handling, deposits and billing cycles must come from CEO SUITE commercial rules or live billing data.

## Business Culture
Business culture is relationship-oriented, service-focused and generally English-friendly. Users may appreciate warm communication, clear follow-up, flexible meeting support and practical visitor-arrival instructions.

## Compliance Considerations
Verify SEC registration, BIR tax registration, local business permits, foreign ownership limits, employment permits, data privacy obligations and sector-specific licences. Do not provide legal or tax advice.

## Visa Overview
Foreign nationals working in the Philippines generally require appropriate work authorization and immigration status. Business visits, local employment and investor roles are different and should be checked with official immigration and labour guidance.
