# Product: Dedicated Workstation

## Product Summary

Dedicated Workstation provides members with a permanently assigned desk within a shared coworking environment.

Unlike Hot Desk memberships, Dedicated Workstation users retain the same reserved seat at all times during their membership period.

This product is commonly used by:

- remote workers

- consultants

- freelancers

- hybrid employees

- small startup teams

- professionals requiring stable workspace

Dedicated Workstation combines the flexibility of coworking with the consistency of a permanent workstation.

## Workspace Features

## Reserved Desk

Members are assigned a dedicated desk for their exclusive use during the active membership term.

The workstation may typically include:

- work desk

- ergonomic chair

- power access

- lockable storage where available

Desk reassignment may require operational approval.

- prohibited items

## FAQ Quick Answers

## What is the difference between Dedicated Workstation and Hot Desk?

Dedicated Workstation provides a permanently reserved desk, while Hot Desk allows flexible seating within shared areas.

## Can I leave my monitor at the desk?

Usually yes, subject to centre policy and security guidelines.

## Is storage included?

Some packages may include storage or lockers depending on location and package type.

## Can I access the centre after business hours?

Some Dedicated Workstation memberships may include extended or 24/7 access.

## Can I receive mail at the centre?

Mail handling availability depends on package type and centre policy.

## Is Dedicated Workstation suitable for teams?

Small teams may use multiple dedicated workstations, though larger or privacy-sensitive teams may prefer private offices.
