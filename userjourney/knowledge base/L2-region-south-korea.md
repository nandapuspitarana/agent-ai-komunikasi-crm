## Business Environment
South Korea is a major Northeast Asian economy with strengths in technology, manufacturing, finance, consumer brands, gaming, entertainment, life sciences and professional services. Seoul is the dominant corporate hub, with Jongno and Gangnam serving different business communities.

## Company Incorporation Overview
Foreign-invested company establishment can involve foreign investment notification, capital remittance, incorporation registration, business registration and foreign-invested company registration. Requirements vary by structure, sector and investor profile, so use current Invest Korea or official guidance.

## Language
Korean is the official and primary business language. English is used in many international business contexts, but local documentation, filings and government processes often require Korean.

## Currency
South Korean won (KRW).

## Public Holidays
Public holidays commonly include New Year's Day, Seollal, Independence Movement Day, Children's Day, Buddha's Birthday, Memorial Day, Liberation Day, Chuseok, National Foundation Day, Hangeul Day and Christmas. Lunar-calendar holidays and substitute holidays can affect dates, so check the official annual calendar.

## Payment Methods
Common business payment methods include bank transfer, corporate card, invoice settlement and local electronic payment rails. Exact accepted methods, VAT/tax handling, deposits and billing cycles must come from CEO SUITE commercial rules or live billing data.

## Business Culture
Business culture can be formal, hierarchical and relationship-aware. Users may expect punctuality, polished meeting environments, quick written confirmation and precise details about building access, billing and contract terms.

## Compliance Considerations
Verify foreign investment registration, business registration, tax, employment, data/privacy requirements, sector licences and address/office-use rules. Do not provide legal or tax advice.

## Visa Overview
Foreign investors, executives and employees may require visas or status such as corporate investment or employment categories depending on role and activity. Always check current immigration requirements.
