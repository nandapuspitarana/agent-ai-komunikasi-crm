## Business Environment
Thailand is a major ASEAN market for manufacturing, tourism, trading, logistics, consumer goods, finance, professional services and regional operations. Bangkok is the main corporate hub, with Wireless Road and central business districts offering premium business addresses.

## Company Incorporation Overview
Company registration is handled through Thai authorities, with foreign ownership and permitted business activities subject to the Foreign Business Act and other sector rules. Foreign Business License or BOI-related routes may be relevant depending on activity. Do not promise eligibility or approval.

## Language
Thai is the official language and primary language for government processes. English is used in many international business settings in Bangkok, but official filings and local processes may require Thai.

## Currency
Thai baht (THB).

## Public Holidays
Public holidays commonly include New Year's Day, Makha Bucha, Chakri Memorial Day, Songkran, Labour Day, Coronation Day, Visakha Bucha, royal birthday holidays, Buddhist Lent-related holidays, Chulalongkorn Day, Constitution Day and New Year's Eve. Cabinet announcements and bank holidays can affect operations, so check the official annual calendar.

## Payment Methods
Common business payment methods include bank transfer, corporate card, PromptPay/QR rails where accepted and invoice settlement. Exact accepted methods, VAT/tax handling, deposits and billing cycles must come from CEO SUITE commercial rules or live billing data.

## Business Culture
Business culture is relationship-aware, polite and hierarchy-sensitive. Users may value respectful tone, clear next steps, building prestige, practical transport guidance and reassurance around visitor handling.

## Compliance Considerations

## Visa Overview
Foreign nationals working in Thailand generally require appropriate visas and work permits. Business visitors, employees, directors and investors may have different requirements.
