# Service: Company Incorporation

## Service Summary

Company Incorporation assists customers with establishing a legal business entity in the selected jurisdiction.

The service is designed to simplify the registration process and help businesses satisfy basic regulatory requirements.

Availability and requirements vary by country.

## Who Is This Service For

Suitable for:

- startups

- entrepreneurs

- foreign investors

- representative offices converting to local entities

- expanding international businesses

## Typical Incorporation Services

Services may include:

- company name application

- company registration filing

- preparation of standard incorporation documents

- coordination with regulatory authorities

- provision of registered address where eligible

- company secretary appointment where legally required

Actual services vary by jurisdiction.

## What Is Usually Not Included

Unless specifically stated:

- business licences

- regulatory approvals

- bank account approval

- tax exemptions

- immigration approvals

- employment pass approval

- trademark registration

These may require separate services.

## Required Information

Customers may be asked to provide:

- passport or identity card

- proof of residential address

- shareholder information

- director information

- proposed company name

- business activity description

Additional documentation may be required.

## Company Name Approval

Company names are generally subject to government approval.

Approval is not guaranteed.

Names may be rejected if they are:

- already registered

- misleading

- offensive

- restricted by regulation

Alternative names may be required.

## Registered Address

A registered address may be available depending on:

- selected package

- jurisdiction

- compliance approval

The availability of registered office services should always be confirmed during onboarding.

## Timeline Expectations

Simple incorporations may be completed within several business days after all required documents are received.

Government processing times vary.

Complex applications may require longer review periods.

The AI assistant must never guarantee a completion date.

## Compliance & KYC

All customers may be subject to:

- identity verification

- anti-money laundering checks

- sanctions screening

- beneficial ownership review

Services may be declined if compliance requirements are not satisfied.

## Common Questions

## Can you guarantee company approval?

No. Approval is determined by the relevant government authorities.

## Can foreigners incorporate a company?

In many jurisdictions, foreign ownership is permitted, subject to local laws and requirements.

## Do I need to be physically present?

Requirements vary by jurisdiction and specific circumstances.

## Can you help with bank account opening?

Assistance may be available, but bank approval is always at the discretion of the financial institution.

## Can I use a Virtual Office address?

In many cases yes, subject to package eligibility, compliance approval and local regulations.
