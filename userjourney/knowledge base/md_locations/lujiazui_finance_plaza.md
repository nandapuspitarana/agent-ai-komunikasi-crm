SHANGHAI PUDONG, CHINA - COWORKING SPACE, VIRTUAL OFFICE, COMPANY FORMATION
CEO SUITE’s luxurious floor on Level 18 features an exclusive open-air balcony. Located within the world-class development of Lujiazui Free Trade Zone (TDZ) and Shanghai Pilot Free-Trade Zone (PFTZ), the center includes an 11-storey shopping mall, cafes, restaurants, gym and several banks.
