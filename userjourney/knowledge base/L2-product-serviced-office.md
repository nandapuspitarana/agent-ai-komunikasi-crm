# Product: Serviced Office

## Product Summary

Serviced Offices are fully furnished private office suites designed for businesses requiring professional workspace with flexible terms and managed operational support.

This product is commonly used by:

- SMEs

- startups

- regional headquarters

- project teams

- representative offices

- enterprise satellite teams

- professional services firms

Serviced Offices provide move-in-ready workspace without the complexity of traditional office leases.
