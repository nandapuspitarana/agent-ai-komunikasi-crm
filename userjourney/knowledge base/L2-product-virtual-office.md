# Product: Virtual Office

## Product Summary

Virtual Office provides businesses with a professional business address and related support services without requiring a full-time physical office.

This product is commonly used by:

- startups

- remote businesses

- overseas companies

- representative offices

- home-based businesses

- digital businesses

- consultants and freelancers

Virtual Office services help businesses establish a professional presence while maintaining operational flexibility and lower overhead costs.

## Core Services

## Business Address

Customers may use the business address for:

- company correspondence

- marketing materials

- business cards

- website display

- client communications

Usage for company registration or regulated licensing purposes may require additional compliance review.

The address must not be used for:

- illegal activities

- misleading representations

- inventory warehousing

- public walk-in retail operations

## Mail Handling

Incoming standard business mail is accepted during business hours.

Mail services may include:

- mail receipt notification

- onsite mail collection

- temporary mail holding

- optional mail forwarding

Mail handling is intended for normal business correspondence volume.

Excessive mail volume may require service upgrade or operational review.

## Parcel Handling

Reasonable business parcel handling is generally supported.

The following may be restricted:

- oversized deliveries

- pallets

- refrigerated items

- hazardous materials

- cash deliveries

- inventory shipments

- high-volume ecommerce fulfilment

Parcels not collected within a reasonable period may incur storage charges.

## Mail Forwarding

Mail forwarding may be available upon request.

Forwarding options may include:

- ad hoc forwarding

- weekly forwarding

- monthly forwarding

Forwarding charges may include:

- postage

- courier fees

- administrative handling charges

International forwarding availability depends on destination country and courier restrictions.

## Phone Answering Services

Phone answering services may be available as an optional add-on.

Typical features may include:

- dedicated business number

- personalised greeting

- receptionist call answering

- message taking

- message forwarding

- basic call screening

Phone answering is intended for normal business usage and may not function as a full call centre service.

## Workspace & Meeting Room Access

## Workspace Access

Some Virtual Office plans may include limited coworking or lounge access.

Workspace access may vary depending on:

- package tier

- centre location

- operational hours

- availability

Virtual Office plans are primarily address-based services and should not be treated as permanent daily workspace memberships unless specifically included.

## Meeting Room Access

Meeting rooms may be:

- included as monthly credits

- available at member rates

- available on pay-per-use basis

Advance booking is generally recommended.

Meeting room access is subject to:

- availability

- operating hours

- booking policies

## Company Registration & Compliance

## Company Registration Eligibility

Some Virtual Office services may support company registration or registered address usage, subject to:

- local laws

- compliance approval

- KYC verification

- permitted business activity

Approval is not guaranteed.

Certain regulated industries may require additional review or may not be supported.

Examples may include:

- financial services

- cryptocurrency-related businesses

- gambling-related activities

- money lending

- regulated medical services

- high-risk trading activities

The AI assistant must never guarantee registration approval.

Complex or regulated cases should always be escalated to a human advisor.

## Compliance Requirements

Customers may be required to provide:

- government-issued identification

- proof of residential address

- company registration documents

- shareholder/director information

- beneficial ownership information

Additional verification may be required for overseas entities or regulated industries.

Compliance checks may be conducted before service activation.

## Access & Operations

## Business Hours

Virtual Office services are generally supported during standard business operating hours.

Mail collection and onsite support may only be available during staffed hours.

Some centres may offer extended access depending on package type.

## Visitor Handling

Virtual Office customers may receive visitors by appointment.

Reception staff may assist with:

- visitor registration

- guest direction

- meeting room coordination

Walk-in usage without booking may not be permitted.

## Commercial Terms

## Billing Cycle

Virtual Office services are typically billed:

- monthly

- quarterly

- annually

Longer commitments may qualify for promotional pricing.

## Deposit Policy

Deposit requirements may vary depending on:

- payment method

- contract duration

- risk assessment

- jurisdiction

Some plans may not require deposits.

Virtual Office services commonly require a minimum commitment period to activate.

Shorter promotional or trial periods may occasionally be available.

## Cancellation Policy

Cancellation generally requires advance written notice before the next billing cycle.

Early termination charges may apply depending on contract terms and promotions received.

## Upgrade Policy

Customers may usually upgrade to:

- coworking membership

- dedicated workstation

- serviced office

- larger workspace solutions

Upgrades are subject to availability and operational approval.

## Internet & Technology

## Internet Access

Limited guest Wi-Fi access may be available when using onsite facilities.

Dedicated internet services are generally not included within standard Virtual Office plans.

## Digital Services

Some centres may provide:

- member portals

- online booking systems

- digital invoices

- visitor notifications

Feature availability may vary by location.

## Restrictions & Acceptable Use

## Restricted Activities

The following activities are generally prohibited:

- illegal activities

- high-risk regulated operations

- inventory warehousing

- manufacturing

- public retail storefront operations

- excessive parcel handling

- nuisance activities

The operator reserves the right to suspend services for policy violations.

## Workspace Usage Restrictions

Virtual Office services are not intended to function as unrestricted daily physical office occupancy unless explicitly included in the plan.

Frequent onsite operational usage may require upgrade to coworking or serviced office products.

## Is meeting room usage included?

Some plans may include meeting room credits or discounted member rates.

## Can I upgrade later?

Yes, upgrades to coworking or serviced office products are generally possible subject to availability.

## Is mail forwarding available?

Mail forwarding may be available as an optional service and may incur additional charges.
