# Product: Coworking

## Product Summary

Coworking provides flexible shared workspace designed for individuals and small teams seeking collaborative and cost-effective working environments.

Coworking products are commonly used by:

- freelancers

- remote workers

- startups

- hybrid workers

- travelling professionals

- consultants

Coworking prioritises flexibility, convenience and community-based working.

## Membership Types

## Hot Desk

Hot Desk allows members to use any available shared seating within designated coworking areas.

Seats are not permanently assigned.

## Dedicated Desk

Dedicated Desk provides members with a permanently assigned workstation.

Dedicated desks may include:

- reserved seating

- storage space

- extended access privileges

## Day Pass

Day Pass allows short-term access for daily workspace usage.

Day passes may:

- include business-hours access only

- exclude storage

- have limited availability during peak periods

## FAQ Quick Answers

## What is the difference between Hot Desk and Dedicated Desk?

Hot Desk provides flexible shared seating, while Dedicated Desk provides a permanently assigned workstation.

## Can I take calls inside coworking?

Yes, reasonable business calls are generally allowed. Phone booths or meeting rooms are recommended for extended or confidential calls.

## Are phone booths available?

Many centres provide phone booths or quiet areas, subject to availability.

## Can I bring guests?

Guests may be allowed subject to building and membership policies.

## Is coworking suitable for teams?

Small teams may use coworking, though larger or privacy-sensitive teams may prefer private offices.

## Can I work every day from coworking?

Yes, depending on the selected membership plan and access rights.
