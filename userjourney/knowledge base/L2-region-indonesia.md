## Business Environment
Indonesia is Southeast Asia's largest economy by population and a major market for consumer, finance, technology, natural resources, logistics and professional-services activity. Jakarta is the primary corporate and government hub for many domestic and international businesses.

## Company Incorporation Overview
Business licensing and company establishment commonly involve the OSS system and relevant Indonesian authorities. Foreign investment structures, business classifications, licensing, capital requirements and address rules can be sector-specific. Do not promise incorporation, licensing or foreign ownership eligibility without approved local advice.

## Language
Bahasa Indonesia is the official language and primary language for government and legal processes. English is used in many multinational, finance and professional-services settings, especially in Jakarta, but local-language support may be needed for formal filings.

## Currency
Indonesian rupiah (IDR).

## Public Holidays
Public holidays commonly include New Year's Day, Chinese New Year, Nyepi, Good Friday, Eid al-Fitr, Vesak, Ascension holidays, Pancasila Day, Eid al-Adha, Independence Day, Islamic New Year, Prophet's Birthday and Christmas. Indonesia may also declare joint leave days, so operational planning should check the official annual calendar.

## Payment Methods
Common business payment methods include bank transfer, corporate card, QRIS/e-wallet rails in some contexts and invoice settlement. Exact accepted methods, VAT/tax handling, deposits and billing cycles must come from CEO SUITE commercial rules or live billing data.

## Business Culture
Business culture is relationship-oriented and respectful of hierarchy. Users may value warm follow-up, local context, clear directions, help with visitor access and careful explanation of compliance or registration steps.

## Compliance Considerations
Verify OSS/NIB licensing, foreign investment restrictions, sector business codes, tax registration, employment permits, data/privacy rules, address eligibility and any building or local authority requirements. Do not provide legal or tax advice.

## Visa Overview
Foreign nationals working in Indonesia usually require appropriate immigration and work authorisation. Business meetings, employment and investor activity may require different permissions and should be checked with official immigration or local advisers.
