## Business Environment
Hong Kong is a major international finance, trading, legal, logistics and professional-services hub. Users often value Hong Kong for its international connectivity, common-law business environment, deep banking ecosystem and access to Mainland China and wider Asia.

## Company Incorporation Overview
Local company incorporation and registration of non-Hong Kong companies are handled by the Companies Registry, with business registration administered through the Inland Revenue Department. Requirements for registered office address, company secretary, significant controllers and business registration should be checked against current official guidance.

## Language
Chinese and English are official languages. English is widely used in finance, legal, corporate and international business contexts. Cantonese is commonly used locally; Mandarin may be relevant for Mainland China-facing business.

## Currency
Hong Kong dollar (HKD).

## Public Holidays
Hong Kong public holidays commonly include New Year's Day, Lunar New Year, Ching Ming Festival, Good Friday/Easter period, Labour Day, Buddha's Birthday, Tuen Ng Festival, HKSAR Establishment Day, National Day, Chung Yeung Festival, Christmas and Boxing Day. Dates vary annually for lunar-calendar holidays, so check the official government holiday list for the relevant year.

## Payment Methods
Common business payment methods include bank transfer, FPS, cheque, corporate card and invoice settlement. Exact accepted methods, deposits, taxes and billing cycles must come from CEO SUITE commercial rules or live billing data.

## Business Culture
Hong Kong business culture is fast-moving, commercially direct and document-driven. Users often expect concise answers, quick quotation turnaround, strong address prestige and clear explanation of contract, billing and access details.

## Compliance Considerations
Verify Companies Registry requirements, business registration, registered office address rules, company secretary requirements, tax filings, employment obligations, privacy requirements and sector licensing. Do not provide legal or tax advice.

## Visa Overview
Foreign nationals working in Hong Kong generally require the appropriate employment, investment or other immigration permission. Business visits and employment are different categories and should be checked with the Immigration Department.
