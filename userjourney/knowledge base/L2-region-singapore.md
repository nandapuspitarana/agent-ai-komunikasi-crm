## Business Environment
Singapore is a major regional business hub for Southeast Asia, commonly used for regional headquarters, finance, technology, professional services, trading and holding-company structures. Users often value Singapore for its stable legal environment, English-language business setting, strong banking system and international connectivity.

## Company Incorporation Overview
Company incorporation is handled through Singapore's Accounting and Corporate Regulatory Authority (ACRA). Foreigners can register businesses in Singapore, but local residency, nominee/director, work pass and operating requirements must be checked against current ACRA and Ministry of Manpower guidance. CEO SUITE should not guarantee incorporation approval or work-pass eligibility.

## Language
English is the primary business language. Malay, Mandarin and Tamil are also official languages. Most commercial documents, contracts and government portals used by foreign businesses are available in English.

## Currency
Singapore dollar (SGD).

## Public Holidays
Public holidays commonly include New Year's Day, Chinese New Year, Good Friday, Hari Raya Puasa, Labour Day, Vesak Day, Hari Raya Haji, National Day, Deepavali and Christmas. Dates can change each year, especially lunar-calendar holidays, so booking, access and billing workflows should check the official Singapore public holiday calendar for the relevant year.

## Payment Methods
Common business payment methods include bank transfer, corporate card, GIRO, FAST/PayNow and invoice-based settlement. Exact accepted methods, deposits, taxes and billing cycles must come from CEO SUITE commercial rules or live billing data.

## Business Culture
Singapore business culture is punctual, structured and documentation-oriented. Users often expect clear pricing, quick written follow-up, professional meeting facilities and precise confirmation of inclusions, access rules and contract terms.

## Compliance Considerations
Common areas to verify include ACRA registration requirements, registered office or business address eligibility, local resident director requirements, GST/tax obligations, sector licensing, data protection and employment/work-pass compliance. Do not provide legal or tax advice.

## Visa Overview
Foreigners working in Singapore generally require an appropriate Ministry of Manpower work pass. Business travel, employment, relocation and company-director scenarios should be routed to current MOM or immigration guidance.
